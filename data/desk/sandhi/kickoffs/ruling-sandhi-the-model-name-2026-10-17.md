Its name is **Sandhi** (सन्धि), with "the gated states" kept as its description: *Sandhi: the gated states.* Versions continue from the current numbering (the run on the desk at this ruling is Sandhi v3.1).

**The three readings, recorded with the coinage (§1.11).** In Sanskrit grammar, sandhi is the change that occurs at the join between two words, nothing changing inside a word: the gated shape, within a span constant, at the boundary a step. Its plain meaning, junction or joint: the span boundary itself. Its cousin sandhyā, the twilight, the join of day and night: the calibration stamp, across which a third of the chip moves and the split on 143 did not. None of the three names a cause; the model's first carried sentence stands: it is a kinetic description.
